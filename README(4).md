# Rescue and Restoration Work in Indian Railway for Accidents in Tunnel

![Tunnel rescue concept](images/tunnel-rescue-cover.png)

A project on **rescue and restoration operations for railway tunnel accidents in Indian Railways**, with a focus on the special challenges of confined spaces, poor ventilation, limited access, and restricted use of heavy equipment.

This work explores two major needs:

- **Human rescue in tunnels** using rope-based rescue and a proposed **I-beam-supported rescue system**
- **Rolling stock recovery** using a **material trolley** designed for derailed coach and wagon restoration in tunnel conditions

---

## Project Highlights

- Study of tunnel accident challenges in Indian Railways
- Review of available disaster management equipment
- Rope rescue methodology for tunnel environments
- Human chain fallback method when rope rescue is not possible
- Proposed **ISMB-125 I-beam rescue system**
- Proposed **material trolley** for derailed rolling stock recovery
- Safety and structural analysis based on engineering standards

---

## Problem Statement

Railway tunnel accidents are difficult to manage because tunnels often have:

- narrow access paths
- poor lighting and ventilation
- communication issues
- limited space for cranes and heavy machinery
- slow evacuation and restoration time

This project aims to improve rescue readiness and restoration efficiency in such conditions.

---

## Proposed Solutions

### 1) Rope Rescue System
A practical rescue method for tunnel emergencies where trained teams can use rope access, anchoring, lowering, hauling, and stretcher handling to evacuate injured people.

![Rope rescue setup](images/rope-rescue-setup.png)

### 2) Human Chain Method
A fallback rescue technique for situations where rope equipment is unavailable or access is highly restricted.

### 3) Permanent I-Beam Rescue System
A fixed rescue arrangement using **ISMB-125** beam sections to support rescue operations inside tunnels with better predictability and structural stability.

![I-beam rescue system](images/i-beam-rescue-system.png)

### 4) Material Trolley for Derailed Rolling Stock
A modular trolley designed to help move derailed rolling stock and recovery components in constrained tunnel environments.

![Material trolley](images/material-trolley.png)
